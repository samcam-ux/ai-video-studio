# AI Video Studio — Enterprise Playbook

## What "Enterprise" Means Here

Not a pricing tier on the website. A **direct-sold annual contract** ($25K–$100K/yr)
where one company buys video generation for their whole team. You pitch the
decision-maker, negotiate terms, sign a contract. 20 of these = $1M.

## The Offer

### Standard Enterprise: $36,000/year
- 5,000 videos/month pooled across the company
- Up to 25 user seats
- Custom brand templates (their logo, colors, fonts baked into outputs)
- Priority rendering queue
- Dedicated support channel
- Onboarding + team training session
- Quarterly usage reports

### White-Label / API: $60,000+/year
- Everything in Standard, plus:
- Their branding on the platform (no "AI Video Studio" visible)
- API access to embed generation in their own tools/CRM
- Custom domain
- SLA (uptime guarantee)

### Why the Math Works for Them
A brokerage with 200 agents × 12 listings/year = 2,400 videos/year.
At videographer rates ($500/video) that's $1.2M. At $36K/yr from us,
they save 97% and every listing gets video same-day. **Lead every pitch
with their number, not our features.**

Your cost per video: ~$0.01. Even at 5,000 videos/mo, your infra cost
is ~$50/mo per enterprise client. Margin: 99%+.

## Target List (in priority order)

### Tier 1 — Your Prospect Directory (start here)
1. **Realtors from your Contractors & Contacts sheet** — You already have
   investor/flip/ADU realtors listed. Realtors are the best first target:
   the ROI math is dead simple (every listing needs video), they answer
   their phones, and brokerages concentrate 100+ agents under one
   decision-maker. You don't need a prior relationship — the pitch is
   simple enough for warm-ish outreach.
2. **Spec builders from your directory** — 10 spec builders already on the
   Spec Builders tab. Smaller deal size than production builders, but they
   market every flip and need content constantly.

### Tier 2 — Referral Ladder (your small builders are the key)
3. **Your small builders → their bigger contacts.** Your few small builders
   aren't $36K deals — they're $49–149/mo self-serve customers AND your
   intro source. Small GCs know bigger GCs. They know developers. Ask:
   "Who's the biggest builder you know? Would you intro me?" One intro
   from a trusted sub beats 100 cold emails.
4. **Ibrahem, Naga, and your engineer contacts** — They work with bigger
   firms and developers. Same ask: intro to the decision-maker, not a pitch.
   Engineers open doors; you walk through them.

### Tier 3 — Agencies & Brokerages
5. **Marketing agencies** (especially real estate/construction vertical) —
   White-label play: they resell to their clients, you power it invisibly.
   One agency = dozens of end-clients.
6. **Regional brokerages** (Keller Williams, RE/MAX, Coldwell Banker offices) —
   100-500 agents each. Pitch the broker/owner. Harder without a warm intro,
   so work these after you have 2-3 customer logos from Tiers 1–2.

## Outreach Sequence (per prospect)

**Week 1 — Warm intro or direct outreach**
- Warm: "Hey [name], built something I think [company] would love — 2-min demo?"
- Cold (LinkedIn/email to marketing VP or broker/owner):
  > Subject: Video for every listing, same day
  > [Name] — quick question: what does [company] currently spend on listing
  > videos? I built a tool that generates them in 2 minutes for about 3% of
  > videographer cost. Worth a 10-min demo on one of your actual listings?

**Week 2 — The demo (this closes deals)**
- Generate a video from THEIR listing/project live. Not a template. Their stuff.
- Show the before: "this took your videographer 5 days and $800"
- Show the after: "this took 2 minutes"
- Then price: "For less than 3 videographer visits, your whole team gets
  5,000 videos a month for a year."

**Week 3 — Follow-up & close**
- Send 2-3 more sample videos from their listings (free, unsolicited)
- Proposal: 1-page, their ROI math at the top, terms below
- Offer pilot: "Start with 90 days at $9K, roll into annual if your team uses it"
  (reduces risk, gets you in the door)

- Don't lead with AI/tech — lead with their cost savings
- Don't offer free pilots longer than 90 days — it signals low value
- Don't discount below $25K/yr — it breaks the positioning
- Don't build custom features for one client before they sign — sell what's live
