## 30-Day Content Plan (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

Post 3-4x/week. Every video follows the same formula: **prompt on screen → generated clip → reaction.** No face needed.

### Week 1 — "Watch this sentence become a movie"
1. Prompt: "lone astronaut walking through neon Tokyo rain" → show the clip. Caption: "I typed one sentence."
2. Same prompt, toggle prompt-enhancement OFF vs ON. Side-by-side. Caption: "The AI rewrote my prompt and the difference is insane."
3. "Things I'd film if I had a Hollywood budget (part 1)" — 3 rapid clips.

### Week 2 — Dialogue feature showcase
4. Character says something funny/unexpected via the dialogue feature. Caption: "I made an AI character say ___"
5. "POV: your movie trailer narrator" — dramatic prompt + dramatic dialogue line.
6. Duet/stitch trend: take a trending sound, generate a matching visual.

### Week 3 — Niche content (pick your lane)
7. Real estate: "listing photos are dead, this is the future" — generate a luxury home tour clip.
8. Small business: "your $0 ad creative" — product-style clip for a generic business.
9. "AI movie posters come to life" — famous movie-poster-style prompts.

### Week 4 — Social proof + offer
10. Compilation: "best things you guys made this month" (once you have users).
11. Price comparison: "$15/mo vs $9 once" — show the math on screen.
12. "3 free videos, no card" — direct CTA post.

### Posting checklist per video
- Hook in first 1 second (the prompt text on screen IS the hook)
- Trending audio underneath (low volume)
- Caption with CTA: "link in bio — 3 free videos"
- Hashtags: #aivideo #texttovideo #aivideogenerator #contentcreator #filmmaking
- Post natively to TikTok, Reels, AND Shorts (same file, 3x reach)
